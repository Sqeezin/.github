## Shorten any link. Free, no ads.

Most free link shorteners make their money on you: an ad page before the link opens, a signup
wall, or click tracking sold on. And nobody can tell where a short link goes before tapping it.

**Squeezin** is a link shortener that is free and stays that way: no ads, no signup, no tracking sold.

- **Paste, done.** A long link becomes `squeez.in/xK7q2`, an easy-to-say `squeez.in/blue-otter`,
  or your own ending. QR codes, expiry (24 hours, 7 days or after the first click) and 30 days
  of click counts are free.
- **Check before you open.** Add `+` to any squeez.in link to see where it goes, who made it,
  and whether Google Safe Browsing or other people have flagged it.
- **Works with your agents.** An MCP server at `squeez.in/mcp` lets an agent create, check and
  expire links and hear when one is opened. Every agent-made link names its agent.

Free is the whole product. A handle (`squeez.in/yourname`) will be $2 once; Pro, with your own
domain and editable links, $3 a month; agents pay per use. No more dodging ad pages or
explaining to people that a link is safe: it takes one paste, and the `+` page answers the
question for you.
